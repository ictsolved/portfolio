Munex

2025-Present
Personal Flutter Android

Financial decision engine for irregular income — safe-to-spend limits, cash flow forecasting, and financial resilience.

Munex is a personal finance app built with Flutter, designed specifically for freelancers and anyone with variable income. It calculates a daily safe-to-spend limit, forecasts cash flow, and helps users build financial resilience without relying on fixed-salary budgeting assumptions.
