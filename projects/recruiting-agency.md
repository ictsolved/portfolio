The Manpower System is a SaaS HR management platform built for recruiting agencies. It handles the full recruitment lifecycle: job postings, candidate pipelines, document management, interview scheduling, deployment tracking, and client reporting.

Built with ASP.NET Blazor on the frontend and PostgreSQL as the data store, the application is served behind Nginx and deployed on a VPS. It is currently live and in production use by a recruiting agency client.
