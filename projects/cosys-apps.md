CoSys Apps is a suite of Flutter mobile applications serving the cooperative banking sector across Nepal. The flagship product is a mobile banking app built on a single Flutter codebase with 100+ white-label flavors — each flavor is customised per cooperative bank using app flavors and a CI/CD pipeline that automates builds and deployments.

The suite also includes executive reporting apps for branch managers and a Branchless Banking (BLB) app used by field collectors and agents to process transactions and collect payments in the field, including integration with POS payment devices.
